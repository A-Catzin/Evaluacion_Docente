# Finalize another coordinator's initial observation draft with an audited admin action

## Intent

Provide a reusable, human-confirmed way for a currently authenticated superadmin to finalize a saved **initial versioned observation** draft created by another coordinator. The final submission must identify the superadmin as the actual submitter and preserve the draft owner's identity as separate provenance. This resolves an operational dead end without impersonating the coordinator, granting the assistant an admin session, or bypassing existing observation rules. This proposal authorizes **no live submission**.

## Product outcome and scope

A superadmin can locate an eligible saved draft, review its target, answers, evidence, ownership and expected result, then explicitly confirm finalization in their own session. With full validation and no other actor's matching initial draft for the same teacher/cycle/version, the action creates exactly one valid initial final submission with a discoverable audit trail and may clean up the selected draft as normal. Otherwise it fails closed atomically, leaving drafts and final state unchanged. The owner and administrators can distinguish who authored the draft from who submitted the final observation, subject to existing authorized visibility.

- **In:** An admin-only review/confirmation experience and a server-side, authenticated, transactional finalization path for another coordinator's saved initial observation draft. Preserve existing scoring, assignment, active-version, window, uniqueness, authorization, audit, RLS and immutable-final invariants. Require an exact draft identity/revision check and fail closed when the draft, permissions, window or observation state changes before confirmation. **No admin override** for an expired window or a lost assignment.
- **In:** Remove only irrelevant `na_reason` fields attached to numeric answers when preparing a submission, retaining every numeric value, genuine N/A reason, evidence entry and other substantive draft content. Prevent the ordinary capture form from retaining hidden stale N/A reasons after a user switches to a numeric score. Out-of-range scores and incomplete or otherwise invalid drafts must still be rejected, never silently repaired.
- **In (instrument boundary):** Eligible **initial versioned** drafts across traditional Escolarizado, Ejecutivo and Virtual instruments **and** the four newer formats. The blocked example is `observation_escolarizado` v1.2: traditional does not mean unversioned. Apply the same eligibility, attribution and validation rules to all seven format families, subject to each active instrument's rules.
- **Out:** Only **unversioned legacy observation tables**, reevaluations and revision drafts are excluded from the observation-draft scope. Also out: bulk operations, impersonation, backdating, manual score overrides, direct production-data fixes, new owner notifications and automatic submission on behalf of an administrator. Existing initial capture remains available to its authorized actors; this flow does not relax it.

## Approved product decisions and confirmation

- **Competing drafts:** If **any other actor** has a matching initial draft for the teacher/cycle/version, do not insert a final or delete drafts; fail closed atomically, even if the admin saw a different draft set during review. No admin override or consent to delete another actor's saved work. With no other actor's matching draft, the selected draft may be cleaned up on successful finalization.
- **Confirmation:** Show the target teacher, cycle and instrument; draft owner versus submitting admin; expected score after permitted normalization; previous/current observation result, if any; and the count of stale numeric-answer N/A reasons to strip. The admin must confirm after reviewing these effects. Do not change scores or evidence during normalization. A conflicting final/revision is a rejection, never an overwrite or hidden replacement.
- **Owner visibility:** The original owner retains access to final attribution and result **only where existing authorization permits**; do not expand visibility or add a notification.
- **Post-commit recovery:** If downstream score recalculation fails **after the final has committed**, retain the immutable final, show a nonduplicate recoverable warning and raise an operational alert for follow-up. Never re-submit the final or claim that the committed record was rolled back. A failure before commit must instead leave no final or draft cleanup.

## Current-state gap and affected areas

The initial versioned-observation draft access path is actor-bound, and existing submission records the authenticated caller. There is no admin action to finalize another actor's draft while keeping both identities distinct. A read-only aggregate of the blocked case suggests valid integer scores and complete section evidence alongside stale nonblank N/A reasons attached to numeric answers. The capture client can hide those reasons without clearing them, while the SQL submission validation rejects numeric answers carrying nonblank reasons. This is a plausible contributing failure, **not a confirmed diagnosis of the failed request**: its POST payload was not observed, and the live RPC definition could not be freshly checked because temporary Supabase SQL authentication was blocked. Do not rerun that blocked SQL CLI path as part of this proposal.

Likely affected areas: admin UI and API authorization, observation draft/capture UI and validation, transactional Supabase RPC and its grants/RLS, immutable submission and draft lifecycle, audit provenance, score recalculation/operational visibility, and targeted regression tests. The final submission may remove the selected draft and change the teacher's calculated observation score; the confirmation UI must disclose those effects before acting. **Source risk:** migration `091_observation_submission_idempotency.sql` defines an `AFTER INSERT` trigger that deletes **all** matching drafts by teacher/cycle/version, without filtering by actor. Therefore an ordinary check of competing drafts before insert is not enough: design must prove that concurrent creation or change of matching drafts cannot allow this trigger to delete another actor's saved work. Establish a fail-closed, race-safe serialization/locking boundary and its proof **before inserting**; a changed draft set must abort without final or cleanup. Do not include real case identifiers or response data in artifacts.

## Safety and acceptance criteria

1. Only an active, authenticated superadmin can initiate and confirm an admin finalization; the database rechecks actor role, assignment/observation eligibility, time window and version state using trusted session identity. An expired window or lost assignment blocks finalization with no admin exception. A client-provided actor ID cannot choose `submitted_by` or bypass RLS. The original coordinator is recorded separately as draft provenance in an explicit, reviewable audit/submission association, not presented as the final submitter; visibility for the original owner stays within existing authorization.
2. The admin reviews the exact draft revision and the approved confirmation details before explicitly confirming. A stale or missing draft, concurrent owner edit, disallowed actor, expired window, inactive version, existing newer final/revision, invalid data, assignment change or **any other actor's matching initial draft** fails without a new final, draft deletion, misleading success audit or partial transactional effects. A changed matching draft set since review requires a fresh review; never treat an earlier no-competitor check as approval. No real actor identifiers belong in this proposal.
3. Validation enforces instrument item membership, full unique answers and evidence, true N/A policy, integer scale bounds, scoring and observation uniqueness. Normalization strips only stale `na_reason` on numeric answers; the confirmation states the count stripped and the persisted final reflects the reviewed scores and evidence. A normal capture form clears the same stale reason at the source.
4. Atomic submission, provenance/audit and permitted selected-draft cleanup ensure retries return the same result only for the **same confirmed operation**; a different actor, draft revision or conflicting final never receives an apparently successful idempotent response. Before implementation, design and verify serialization/locking against **concurrent inserts and edits of other actors' matching drafts before the final insert and through migration091 trigger cleanup**. If another actor's matching draft exists or appears under a race, abort without inserting or deleting any drafts; the trigger must never erase that actor's work. Do not rely on a nonlocking preflight count. On successful finalization with no competing actor, selected-draft cleanup is allowed.
5. Targeted tests cover `observation_escolarizado` v1.2 as a traditional versioned initial draft, eligibility across Escolarizado/Ejecutivo/Virtual and four newer formats, and exclusion of unversioned legacy tables, reevaluations and revision drafts. Test authorized success, authenticated submitter and separate original-author provenance, existing-authorization-only owner visibility, numeric-reason cleanup and its displayed count, ordinary-form prevention, invalid/out-of-range rejection, window/assignment denial, double-click/retry, concurrent draft mutation and final/revision races, and rollback on pre-commit transactional failure. Specifically test **zero, one and multiple other actors' matching drafts**, plus a changed draft set between review and confirmation and races with competing draft creation/edit before insert: success only with zero other actors; with one, multiple or changed/racing sets, no final, draft deletion or partial audit. Test downstream recalculation failure **after commit** separately: the immutable final remains, the warning is nonduplicate and recoverable, an operational alert is raised, and recovery neither re-submits nor claims rollback.

## Risks and rollback

The highest risks are an admin final attributed to the wrong person, an unintended score change, **deletion of another coordinator's matching draft by the existing migration091 trigger**, and a duplicate or partial finalization under concurrency. The product policy is approved: **fail closed when any other actor has a matching initial draft**. Its race-safe enforcement and proof under the trigger remain a **design and verification gate before implementation**, not permission to proceed with a simple preflight check. Keep the operation narrowly scoped, confirm it with a human in the admin session, revalidate and serialize affected state at the database boundary, retain provenance, and expose recoverable failure states. A later implementation may disable the admin entry point and revoke its execution grant if unsafe; already committed finals and their audit records are immutable and **must not be presented as reversible by application rollback**; deployment rollback also cannot restore drafts removed by a committed cleanup. Remediation for a wrong final requires the separately authorized correction process, not deletion or fabrication of history. Database migration rollback and deployment must be reviewed against live dependencies before use; the current SQL-auth blocker remains unresolved.
