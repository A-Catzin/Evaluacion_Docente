# Route approved student evaluations to CD-07

## Intent and outcome

The production rollout serves CD-07 instead of the general student form for exactly the 20 approved real cultural/sport class assignments. All other assignments retain the general form. Separately, an isolated, explicit opt-in production fixture path may be authorized for a newly created TEST-CD test course and the separately verified test student and teacher. TEST-CD is never part of the 20-class rollout and its submissions never contribute to institutional results. This closes the gap between the detached CD-07 draft and the live 19-answer route without changing historical general-form results.

**Activation is blocked:** the selected 20-group CSV-to-app assignment reconciliation does not prove student enrollment/role or authorize routing. The signed owner-approved mapping and remaining runtime gates are still required. No IDs from the other application are valid evidence. Do not infer eligibility from a displayed course name or active-list appearance.

The real production rollout candidate set is **20 evaluable assignments from 26 names**: four absent classes, unassigned PINTURA TUP (B), and AJEDREZ TUP (A) (app-assigned but absent from the CSV, zero rows) are excluded. TEST-CD is a distinct fixture exception, not a twenty-first real evaluable class and not part of the 20. The CSV-to-app matching is 20/20, but student enrollment and signed activation mapping remain unresolved; this document intentionally contains no invented roster, account identifiers, emails, or real answers.

## Proposed behavior and affected areas

1. Reconcile the approved roster with the authoritative 27-1 assignments, teacher-selection mapping, enrollment, and cycle before activation. The server returns the selected instrument only for verified eligible assignments; missing, conflicting, or stale mapping fails closed to no CD-07 access, not an alternate client-chosen form.
2. Keep the dashboard/teacher-selection flow and existing general evaluation usable. Route a verified CD-07 assignment to its versioned 20-answer form; preserve the 19-answer endpoint/validator for general evaluations and introduce an explicitly versioned contract for CD-07 rather than silently changing its payload.
3. The UI must not offer N/A for any of the 20 quantitative items; both API and database must reject N/A, null/blank, `na_reason`, and alternate nonnumeric codes without partial effects. At the trusted API/database boundary, derive student identity from the authenticated session and check role, enrollment, selected group/teacher, cycle, instrument version, answer count/range, comment length/moderation, and prior submission. Commit answers and submission control atomically; retries/duplicates must not produce a second response or partial score update. Preserve attribution/audit information without putting identifying details in OpenSpec.
4. Store CD-07 responses alongside, not over, historic general responses using a forward-only, non-destructive versioned persistence path. Normalize the mean of 20 values to 0–100 as `(mean - 1) * 25`, consistent with 1–5 endpoint semantics; combine with the existing student indicator using valid response counts, subject to verification against the deployed scoring contract. The real rollout remains limited to the reconciled 20 assignments. Treat TEST-CD as a separate fixture route: availability requires trusted TEST-CD course identity, authenticated verified test student and enrollment/teacher assignment, active cycle, a feature gate, and a separately authorized production write plan. A feature gate is a design option, not an assertion that one is already implemented. Fail closed if trusted identity mapping or any authorization is missing; never permit names/IDs supplied by a client to establish fixture eligibility. Exclude TEST-CD by trusted course identity at every institutional aggregation and export boundary, including denominators and recalculation; its active-list visibility does not imply report inclusion.

Likely affected source areas are `src/pages/estudiante/dashboard.astro`, `src/pages/estudiante/evaluar/[grupoId].astro`, `src/pages/api/estudiante/evaluaciones.ts` (preserved general contract plus CD-07 entry point), `src/lib/studentEvaluations.ts`, `src/lib/validation/apiSchemas.ts`, `src/lib/moderation.ts`, `src/lib/studentEvaluationDraftForms.ts`, `src/services/scoring.ts`, related report/export consumers, and a new reviewed migration under `supabase/migrations/`. The live evaluation-list and submission RPC definitions and their deployed state require authorized verification. Coordinate migration/deployment sequencing with the separate `audited-admin-observation-finalization` change; do not fold admin-observation behavior into this scope.

## Risks, privacy, and recovery

- **Wrong exposure or authorization:** teacher selection alone does not grant eligibility. Server-side assignment and enrollment are authoritative; deny ambiguous or inactive selections. Verify role and original authenticated actor at write time, and avoid leaking other students' submissions or private comments through routes, lists, reports, or logs.
- **Duplicates or data loss:** enforce a database-backed one-time invariant for student/group/cycle across instrument versions, with transactional failure and retry handling. Preserve general history and do not run, recreate, or adapt destructive migration 043. Confirm deployed migration state, constraints, and backups through a separately authorized operational review before migration.
- **Misreported scores:** confirm compatibility of 0–100 normalization, valid-count weighting, and all downstream consumers. TEST-CD exclusion must hold for teacher-level and subject-level scores, reports, exports, denominators, and recalculation even if the test course is listed as active. Do not put test responses in production SDD artifacts.
- **Rollback:** stage migration and routing activation separately. If routing or scoring fails, disable CD-07 exposure/submission and revert application paths to the general-only behavior for unaffected classes; retain already committed CD-07 data and audit trail. Application rollback does not undo a committed schema change or submission. Any correction to persisted data requires a reviewed, authorized forward recovery plan, not deletion or a replay of 043.

Feature gating is a design option and must not be represented as already implemented.
